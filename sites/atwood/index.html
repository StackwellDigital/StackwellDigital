What we use

Material honestly selected.

Black Walnut
Rich, dark tones that deepen with age. Our most requested species for dining and bedroom furniture.
White Oak
Warm, versatile, and hardwearing. Takes stain beautifully and suits almost every interior style.
Maple
Clean, pale, and tight-grained. Ideal for pieces where the form is meant to speak louder than the wood.
Douglas Fir
Our local BC species. Sustainably sourced, characterful, and deeply connected to where we live.
How it works

From sketch
to your home.

Every piece we build starts with a conversation. We want to understand how you live before we make anything.

i.
Consultation
We meet in person or over video to discuss your space, your needs, and the pieces you're drawn to.
ii.
Design & Approval
We produce detailed drawings and material samples. You approve before anything is cut.
iii.
Build
Your piece is built in our studio. You'll receive progress photos throughout the 10–14 week process.
iv.
White Glove Delivery
We deliver and install every piece ourselves. No couriers, no flat-pack, no surprises.
