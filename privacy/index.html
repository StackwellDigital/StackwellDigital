Privacy Policy

We don't sell your data. We don't track you. Here's exactly what we do with your information.

Last Updated: May 2026

Overview

Stackwell Digital ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our services.

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Period.

Information We Collect

When You Subscribe

We collect information necessary to provide our services:

  • Your name
  • Business name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Billing address
  • Payment information (processed securely by Square)

When You Use Your Site

We collect minimal technical data:

  • Server logs (IP address, browser type, pages accessed)
  • Site performance metrics (uptime, load time, errors)
  • Backup and recovery metadata

We do not collect:

  • Cookies from your site visitors
  • Behavioral tracking data
  • Location data
  • Device fingerprinting
  • Visitor analytics beyond basic server logs

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only for:

  • Providing and maintaining your website hosting
  • Billing and payment processing
  • Customer support and technical assistance
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Compliance with legal obligations

We will never use your information for:

  • Marketing or advertising to third parties
  • Selling or licensing your data
  • Profiling or behavioral analysis
  • Creating audience segments for resale
  • Training AI models or machine learning systems

Third-Party Services

We use only essential third-party services:

Payment Processing

Square, Inc. — Processes your monthly subscription payments. Square's privacy policy is available at https://squareup.com/legal/privacy

Email Delivery

Google Workspace — Hosts your email and ours. Google's privacy policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy

DNS & Hosting

Cloudflare — Manages your domain DNS and site infrastructure. Cloudflare's privacy policy is available at https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

We have selected these providers based on their strong privacy practices and commitment to data protection. We do not authorize them to use your data for any purpose beyond providing their services.

Data Storage & Security

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your website and backups are stored on Cloudflare's servers in North America. Your account data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption.

How We Protect Your Data

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher
  • Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text
  • Access to your data is restricted to authorized Stackwell Digital personnel only
  • We perform regular security audits and penetration testing
  • We maintain offsite encrypted backups for disaster recovery

Data Breach Notification

If we discover unauthorized access to your personal data, we will notify you within 48 hours via email. We will provide details about what data was accessed and steps we're taking to secure it.

Your Rights

Access & Portability

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Request correction of inaccurate information

Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data. Upon cancellation of your account:

  • Your website is taken offline immediately
  • Your personal information is flagged for deletion
  • Your data is permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Backups are retained for 60 days for disaster recovery, then deleted

Note: We are required by law to retain billing records for 7 years for tax purposes. These records contain only your name, email, billing address, and transaction amounts — not your website content.

Opt-Out

You can opt out of non-essential communications by replying "UNSUBSCRIBE" to any email from us.

Visitor Data & Your Responsibility

Your website may collect visitor data (contact forms, email signups, etc.). You are responsible for:

  • Clearly disclosing what data you collect
  • Obtaining explicit consent before collecting personal information
  • Providing a privacy policy on your website if you collect visitor data
  • Complying with applicable privacy laws (PIPEDA in Canada, GDPR for EU visitors, etc.)

We do not access, use, or have responsibility for visitor data collected through your website. That data belongs to you.

Cookies & Tracking

On stackwell.digital

We do not use cookies or tracking pixels. We use no analytics services (Google Analytics, Hotjar, etc.). We don't know how many people visit our site, and we don't care to.

On Your Site

Your website does not include any tracking code by default. If you want to add analytics, you can integrate it yourself (Google Analytics, Plausible, Fathom, etc.). You are responsible for disclosing analytics collection to your visitors.

Legal Obligations

We may disclose your information when required by law:

  • Court orders or subpoenas
  • Government investigations
  • Law enforcement requests
  • Protection of legal rights or safety

We will not voluntarily provide data to government agencies without legal process. We will challenge overly broad requests when possible.

International Users

If you access our services from outside Canada:

  • Your data is stored in North America (Cloudflare's servers)
  • Your data is subject to this privacy policy and Canadian privacy law
  • By using our services, you consent to data transfer and storage in North America
  • We comply with GDPR requirements for EU users (data processing agreements available on request)

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided information, we will delete it immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy at any time. Changes take effect immediately. We will notify you of material changes via email or by posting a notice on our website.
